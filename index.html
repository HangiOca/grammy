A black cat spits red magic from its jaws and strikes back every time you hurt it.

A moonlit library where Grammy trains. Learn the Magical Shield and Light Thunder before the next boss.

  • Magical Shield: blocks a magical hit, recharges every 11 s

CHAPTER 6 · PRISM SANCTUM IN THE SKY

A silver-haired prophetess angel with crystal wings and floating prisms. Control and traps: she turns the whole arena into a mirror maze.

★★★★☆
CHAPTER 7 · THE STORM SKY

Vaelthor — The Storm Seraph

A majestic angel mage in black-silver armor and a white cloak, with huge wings veined by white-blue lightning and a cracked halo behind him.

  • Lightning Orb, Chain Judgment, Storm Pillars

  • The longer the fight, the stronger the storm
  • Weakness: drained of energy after the big storm — counterattack
★★★★★
CHAPTER 8 · THE DIVINE SINGULARITY

Elysara — The Divine Singularity

A supreme angel mage with six white-gold wings and magic circles turning around her body, holding a ball of compressed light that bends space.

  • Mixes several spells at once; phases at 66% and 33%

THE STORY SO FAR

Eight chapters, eight bosses — and one skill room in between. Defeat each boss and press “Next chapter”.

  1. 1 · The Iron Sentinel — Modern City
    A steel android guards a night plaza. Grammy learns to dodge its slams and, at half health, its red magic arrow volleys.
  2. 2 · The Giant Red Cat — Enchanted Forest
    A cat that starts annoyed and turns furious at 50%: faster, stronger, and every hit carries 50% extra red magic. Played in hard mode.
  3. 3 · The Giant Rock Monster — Moonlit Field
    A grey stone giant veined with gold under a moon and fireflies. Slams deal 15%; at 25% it leaps and sends earthquake waves, so Grammy learns to jump (Space).
  4. 4 · The Shadow Cat — Cursed Moor
    A black cat that spits red magic and strikes back when hurt. At 50% its heart booms (44% within 4 m) and Grammy bleeds; red earth spirits every 8 s; at 25% it leaps for a 55% quake in 8 m.
  5. ✦ · Interlude: The Skill Sanctum — Moonlit library
    Grammy trains and learns the Magical Shield (blocks one magical hit, every 11 s) and Light Thunder (22% chance to crit for double damage). Space now auto-jumps.
  6. 5 · Solarius, the Golden Colossus — Union of Angels
    A golden giant with a Divine Greatsword: Radiant Cleave, Solar Charge, Judgment Field and the Sunfall ultimate. He is stunned after every slam, and the shield blocks his light magic.
  7. 6 · Seraphine, the Prism Oracle — Prism sanctum in the sky
    A crystal-winged prophetess who turns the arena into a mirror maze: Prism Shards, Refraction Beam, Crystal Cage and Spectrum Collapse. Break her crystals to cut her reflections.
  8. 7 · Vaelthor, the Storm Seraph — Storm sky
    A lightning angel mage: Lightning Orb, Chain Judgment, Storm Pillars and Heaven’s Thunder. The longer the fight, the stronger the storm. Find the safe zone, then counterattack when he is drained.
  9. 8 · Elysara, the Divine Singularity — Cosmic sanctum (final boss)
    A six-winged supreme angel mage with Holy Comets, Gravity Halo, Divine Ray and the Singularity of Heaven. Break her magic circles in the correct order to stop the final explosion.
  10. ✦ · The Magical Learning Sanctuary — Bonus skill hub
    Grammy learns Prism Shards from Seraphine, Lightning Orb and Storm Pillars from Vaelthor, and Holy Comets from Elysara, then tests them on practice dummies. They work in every chapter on keys 5–8.

HOW TO PLAY

Use a computer with a keyboard. The camera follows you and stays locked on the boss.

W SMove toward / away from the boss
A DStrafe left / right
1 2 3Cast your three skills (they use mana)
SpaceJump — Levels 3 and 4 (jump over earthquake waves)
HDrink the HP Potion (square at the right of the screen): heals 50%, 22 s cooldown
Low healthBelow 25% health Grammy moves 11% faster. Earth Spirit [3] now stuns bosses much longer.
5–8Boss skills learned in the Magical Learning Sanctuary: Prism Shards, Lightning Orb, Storm Pillars, Holy Comets
Z X C VSkills bought in the Cash Shop
Quality buttonTop-right in Levels 2–4. “8K” renders at up to 7680 px wide and needs a strong graphics card.

Every boss changes its behaviour as its health falls. Watch the warning rings on the ground and read the on-screen messages: most deadly attacks are telegraphed. Stay mobile, spend your mana wisely, and jump when the ground shakes.

Needs internet (loads the 3D engine) and a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

YOUR SKILLS

The same three spells in every battle.

1 · Magical Spotlight

A pillar of light falls on the boss for heavy instant damage.

2 · Fire Spirit

A homing fireball that burns the boss for three seconds.

3 · Earth Spirit

Rock spikes burst from the ground toward the boss and stagger it.

Space · Jump

Leap over shockwaves. Timing is everything in Levels 3 and 4.
